# Hex Stack

Turn the six-sided stack under a falling tile. Match three touching colors to clear them before one side fills.

Rotate with ←/→ or A/D. Space drops the tile early. P or Escape pauses. On touch screens use the three buttons below the board.

The turning-stack matching loop was researched in [Hextris](https://github.com/Hextris/hextris) (GPL-3.0-or-later; license: [LICENSE.md](https://github.com/Hextris/hextris/blob/master/LICENSE.md)). This is an independent HVN implementation: no upstream code, art, audio, fonts, or bundled assets are used.

Design read: a compact tabletop puzzle with a dark ink board, flat colored tiles, and a single clear rotation choice. Controls stay beside the board on a phone and beside the scores on a wide screen. No glow, cabinet frame, or decorative telemetry.
