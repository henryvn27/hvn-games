# Neon Bastion — encounter plan

| Wave | Mix | New pressure | Player answer |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | scouts | route timing | one Arc or Rail tower |
| 2 | scouts + brutes | health versus speed | pair short and long range |
| 3 | scouts + brutes + phase runners | a fast leak threat | Pulse slow field or a second Arc |
| 4+ | weighted mix, capped per wave | density and shorter gaps | upgrade a role, then cover a second approach |

Enemies enter one at a time with a visible gap. Brutes are slow and durable; phase runners are quick but fragile. The first three waves introduce one idea at a time, then combine known ideas. Energy is generous enough to recover from an imperfect first placement but scarce enough that upgrades have a real opportunity cost.

## Spatial read

The route bends through the middle of the board. Pads near the first bend favor early damage; pads near the core favor a final safety net. The board uses fixed cells so touch placement is forgiving, and tower range rings make overlap legible without covering the route.
